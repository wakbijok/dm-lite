# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want dm-lite to stay a useful, technical project. Contributors and users
should be able to report bugs, ask questions, and send patches without abuse,
harassment, or bad-faith noise.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Stay on topic: the code, docs, design, and operations of dm-lite
- Assume good intent; disagree on the technical merit
- Keep feedback specific and actionable
- Respect that maintainers have limited time

Examples of behaviour that is not OK:

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment
- Trolling, spam, or off-topic provocation
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualised language or imagery in project spaces
- Sustained disruption of issues, PRs, or discussions

## Scope

This applies in the GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions) and in any
official project spaces the maintainers designate.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainers via a private channel:

- GitHub: open a private security/report path if available, or email the
  address listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and PRs
that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors
for serious or repeated violations.
